# ADR-0003: Waitlist promotion
Status: Accepted | Date: 2026-10-21

## Context
Open decision from the baseline document (D3); resolved in HLD section 3.

## Decision
Automatic promotion with transactional recheck of eligibility and capacity.

## Consequences
Rationale: Avoids offer-expiry state in v1.
Cost accepted: Users may miss a promoted slot.
